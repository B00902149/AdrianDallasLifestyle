Common questions

Questions people ask before booking

What if I've tried everything and nothing has worked?

That's exactly who Adrian works best with. The people who've done every diet, every programme, and every pill — and are ready to finally approach it differently. The coaching is built around why things didn't stick before, not just what to do next.

What if I genuinely don't have much time?

Time is the most common reason people give — and the most fixable. The plan is built around what you actually have, not an ideal schedule. If you have 30 minutes three times a week, that's enough to start making real progress.

What happens on the first call?

No hard sell and no pressure. Adrian will ask about your goals, what you've tried before, and what your life actually looks like. From there you'll know whether working together makes sense — and so will he.
